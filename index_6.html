Te Tiriti o Waitangi in Akoma Ntoso

The Treaty of Waitangi of 1840, marked up in Akoma Ntoso, the OASIS standard for legal and legislative documents. The Māori and English texts are two documents in one collection. They differ in meaning, so neither is marked up as a translation of the other.

View the Treaty

How it was made

The XML was generated by Claude for Grant Vergottini from Schedule 1 of New Zealand’s Treaty of Waitangi Act 1975, which sets out both texts. The signatories’ names and marks are not reproduced.
